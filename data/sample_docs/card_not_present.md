# Card-Not-Present Fraud

## What card-not-present fraud is

Card-not-present (CNP) fraud is the use of stolen card details — the
card number, expiry date, and security code — to buy goods or services
where the physical card is not presented, typically online or by phone.
It became the dominant form of card fraud as chip-and-PIN and
contactless controls made counterfeiting and face-to-face misuse
harder. The merchant never sees the card or the customer, so the
details alone are enough to attempt a transaction.

## Where stolen details come from

Criminals obtain card data from data breaches, phishing pages that
imitate shops and banks, malware on checkout pages (skimming scripts),
and by buying bulk lists on criminal marketplaces. They then test the
details with automated bots in a practice called card testing:
thousands of small authorization attempts
identify which cards are live before the valuable purchases begin.
Credential stuffing targets accounts instead — reused passwords from one
breach are tried against shopping and banking logins, and a successful
login can expose saved cards.

## Defences: 3-D Secure, tokenization, and monitoring

3-D Secure (branded as Visa Secure or Mastercard Identity Check) adds a
step where the card issuer verifies the customer, often with a one-time
passcode or banking-app approval. It cuts CNP fraud substantially and
shifts liability for remaining fraud to the issuer. Tokenization
replaces the real card number with a stand-in token stored by the
merchant, so a breach of the merchant's systems yields nothing reusable.
Issuers also score transactions in real time, flagging mismatched
addresses, unusual locations, velocity of attempts, and first-time
merchants.

## Chargebacks

When a cardholder disputes a fraudulent transaction, the issuer reverses
it through a chargeback and the merchant usually bears the cost, plus a
fee, unless liability shifted under 3-D Secure. High chargeback rates
can cost a merchant its ability to accept cards at all, which is why
online sellers invest in screening tools despite the friction they add
for genuine customers.
